A three-week research assistantship (August 2026) at the [Institute of Communication and Media Studies](https://www.ikmb.unibe.ch/index_eng.html), University of Bern, with [Dr. Mykola Makhortykh](https://www.ikmb.unibe.ch/about_us/people/dr_makhortykh_mykola/index_eng.html).

I built an LLM-based pipeline to classify antisemitic content, following the IHRA definition, in about 30,000 chatbot responses. I compared six LLMs as classifiers (F1 = 0.86). The study also tested how well LLM safety filters hold up against different prompting strategies, such as role-play framing. The findings were presented at the Weizenbaum Institute.

**Methods:** Python, LLM-based classification, LLM evaluation.
